A ten-minute monthly routine

Log month walks you through the same four steps every month, then shows how the month moved your savings rate and net worth. Skip any step; every tool stays available on its own.

  1. IncomeSalary, freelance work and other income, with a copy from last month.
  2. ExpensesPaste the month's spending as plain text and review suggested categories.
  3. InvestmentsUpdate holdings and cash balances, starting from last month's snapshot.
  4. PensionRecord pension values and see the change since last month.

Paste a month of expenses

Write expenses the way you remember them, such as cinema 12.50 or 03/11 groceries 54,20. Fire Tools matches each line to a category with a BM25 search over your own history and a built-in multilingual vocabulary. It runs locally and learns how you file things.

  • Your import rules always win, then your own past expenses, then common terms.
  • Need or want stays your choice for every expense.
  • Optional AI refinement uses the provider you configure: a local model such as Ollama or LM Studio, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Gemini, Anthropic or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Only descriptions and amounts are sent, and only when you ask.

The browser demo uses sample data and does not save edits. Download the local-first desktop app for your own finances, or run its SQLite backend with Docker Compose.

Local-first by design

  • Your data stays local. The desktop app stores everything on your machine, with optional passphrase encryption for the database.
  • No accounts, no analytics. There is nothing to sign up for and no tracking.
  • External calls are explicit. Market prices come from Yahoo Finance; AI categorization only runs with the provider you configure and per-action consent.

macOS: if the app is blocked

Download the macOS DMG or ZIP from the official releases. Open the DMG or extract the ZIP, then move Fire Tools.app into Applications before opening it.

If macOS Gatekeeper blocks the app, first verify that your download came from the official releases above and that you trust it. Only then open Terminal and run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Fire Tools.app"

Reopen Fire Tools from Applications. This removes the quarantine attribute from this app only, bypassing its downloaded-app quarantine checks. Never use this command for an untrusted download or disable Gatekeeper globally.
